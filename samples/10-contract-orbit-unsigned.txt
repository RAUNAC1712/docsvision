VENDOR SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into on 15 September 2026 between Northwind Traders Pvt Ltd ("Buyer") and Orbit Logistics ("Supplier").

WHEREAS the Buyer requires transport and warehousing services, the parties agree as follows:

1. Scope. The Supplier shall provide line-haul transport between Pune and Mumbai.

2. Renewal. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods unless either party gives notice.

3. Fees. Total Contract Value: Rs. 9,60,000.00 per annum.

4. Liability. The liability of the Supplier for loss of goods is limited to the freight charges paid.

5. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.
